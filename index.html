Beginner tips

  • Sit or stand tall, shoulders relaxed, jaw loose.
  • Hum the note before adding words — pitch first, tone second.
  • Start around middle C (C4); it sits comfortably in most voices.
  • Chase the green zone (±5 ¢), not a perfect number.
  • Short daily sessions (5–10 min) beat one long one.
